Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the free Android app developed by YaneDev that provides solutions to the exercises of the 4th Year Middle School (4AM) mathematics textbook (the “App”). By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

The App does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. We do not collect, store or share any personal data ourselves, and we do not run our own servers for user data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays ads served by Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC. AdMob may collect and use certain information automatically in order to show and measure ads, such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. This is handled by Google, not by us, and we do not receive or have access to this data.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads). For more information, see Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses data in AdMob.

3. Third-party services

Apart from Google AdMob described above, the App does not use any other third-party service, analytics tool or SDK that collects data.

4. Children’s privacy

The App is an educational tool for students. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Ads shown in the App are served by AdMob in line with Google’s advertising policies. If you are a parent or guardian and have a concern, please contact us.

5. Data security and retention

Because we do not collect personal data, we do not store any on our side. The App’s content is used only on your device.

It is provided free of charge to everyone, for educational and personal use only. The solutions are provided as a study aid and are not guaranteed to be error-free.

We are not responsible for any illegal, improper or unauthorized use of the App by any person.
